# Password reset and account lockout

## Reset a forgotten password

Use the organization's approved self-service password reset portal if it is available. Verify the portal address independently rather than following links from unexpected messages. If self-service fails, contact the service desk and complete identity verification. Never send your old or new password to support. After a reset, update saved credentials only in approved applications.

## Repeated account lockouts

For repeated account lockouts, stop repeated sign-in attempts. Record the affected account identifier and time without recording the password. The identity team should investigate stale credentials, connected devices and sign-in logs. Unlocking an account requires authorization and identity verification.
